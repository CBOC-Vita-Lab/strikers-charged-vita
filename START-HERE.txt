MARIO STRIKERS CHARGED VITA
Private candidate: a8d4ad6 / MSCN00003. Not cleared for redistribution.

REQUIRED: homebrew-enabled Vita, VitaShell, your own supported disc and
your legitimately obtained libshacccg.suprx in ur0:data/. The module is not
included, even though the VPK has a startup shader cache.

FIRST INSTALL
1. Extract this entire install ZIP on your computer. Do not unzip the VPK.
2. Open CHECK-DOWNLOAD.md to check the download, then PREPARE-GAME.md to
   extract your own Wii USA Rev 1 (R4QE01) disc image.
3. Using VitaShell USB or FTP, copy game/sys and game/files so you have:
     ux0:data/mscharged/game/sys/fst.bin
     ux0:data/mscharged/game/files/Art/
   Do not create game/game or game/DATA. Allow about 3 GB free on the Vita.
4. Copy Strikers-a8d4ad6-private-candidate.vpk to ux0:VPK/ (create VPK if
   needed). End the transfer, then open that VPK in VitaShell and install.
5. Open the Mario Strikers Charged bubble. See CONTROLS.md for the layout.

ALREADY INSTALLED?
Back up ux0:data/mscharged/ first. Install only the matching new player VPK.
Do not delete the bubble, replace individual eboot/shader files, recopy the
game, or replace the whole data folder as an ordinary app update.

KEEP: game/ (your extracted disc), np-data/NAND/ (saves), np-player.txt
      (settings), and your own artwork. Do not use someone else's save.
INCLUDED: player VPK, its matching startup/shader resources, guides and
          an optional offline download checker. No compiler is needed.
NOT INCLUDED: disc image, extracted game, saves, firmware or HD models.

Keep the guides and notices on your computer; do not copy the whole ZIP
contents into ux0:app/. The VPK installer places its own app resources.
INSTALL.md has the full folder map, backup advice and troubleshooting.

The package currently retains private installed artwork and an uncleared
runtime. Do not share it. Fresh-install and source/rights review remain open.
