MARIO STRIKERS CHARGED VITA
Settings preview: 2026-10-19 / MSCN00003. Source/package licensing review and
full installation testing remain pending. Publication is not third-party
approval or a grant of additional redistribution rights.

REQUIRED: homebrew-enabled Vita, VitaShell, your own supported disc and
your legitimately obtained libshacccg.suprx in ur0:data/.

EASIEST: Use CBOC Vita Installer 0.1.10 or newer with the matching release ZIP
or complete folder. Connect your Vita, select your own game, then install.
See DESKTOP-INSTALL.md. No separate Python or Dolphin installation is needed.

MANUAL INSTALL
1. Extract this entire install ZIP on your computer. Do not unzip the VPK.
2. Copy Strikers-settings-20261019.vpk to ux0:VPK/ using VitaShell
   USB or FTP (create VPK if needed). End the transfer, then open that VPK
   in VitaShell and install it.
3. Copy your own Wii USA Rev 1 (R4QE01) game's sys/ and files/ folders to:
     ux0:data/mscharged/game/sys/fst.bin
     ux0:data/mscharged/game/files/Art/
   Do not create game/game or game/DATA. Allow about 3 GB free on the Vita.
4. Open the Mario Strikers Charged bubble. See CONTROLS.md for the layout.
   PS > LiveArea Settings opens settings without restarting your game.

ONLY HAVE AN ISO OR RVZ?
PREPARE-GAME.md shows the Dolphin menu steps to extract it. No Python or
compiling is needed for that route. The Python helper is optional.

ALREADY INSTALLED?
Back up ux0:data/mscharged/ first. Install only the matching new player VPK.
Do not delete the bubble, replace individual eboot/shader files, recopy the
game, or replace the whole data folder as an ordinary app update.

KEEP: game/ (your extracted disc), np-data/NAND/ (saves), np-player.txt
      (settings), and your own artwork. Do not use someone else's save.
INCLUDED: player VPK, matching shaders, generic app art, optional preparation helper,
          source, guides, notices and an optional download checker.
NOT INCLUDED: disc image, extracted game, saves, firmware or HD models.

Keep the guides and notices on your computer; do not copy the whole ZIP
contents into ux0:app/. The VPK installer places its own app resources.
INSTALL.md has the full folder map, backup advice and troubleshooting.

CHECK-DOWNLOAD.md has the optional file check. Technical and release notes
are in BUILDING.md, SOURCE.md and KNOWN-ISSUES.md.
