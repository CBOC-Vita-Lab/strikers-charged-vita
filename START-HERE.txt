MARIO STRIKERS CHARGED FOR PS VITA - VERSION 1.1
================================================

Thanks for downloading! This is a native PS Vita version of Mario Strikers
Charged. You need your own copy of the Wii game; no game data is included.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
- A Vita with homebrew enabled and VitaShell
- Your own Mario Strikers Charged disc: Wii, USA, Rev 1 (R4QE01),
  as an ISO, RVZ or extracted folder
- libshacccg.suprx in ur0:data/ (most homebrew-ready Vitas already have it)
- About 3 GB of free space


EASY INSTALL
------------
CBOC Vita Installer does everything for you on Windows, Mac or Linux:
  github.com/CBOC-Vita-Lab/vita-installer/releases/latest
See EASY-INSTALL.md.


MANUAL INSTALL
--------------
1. Copy Strikers-Charged-Vita.vpk to ux0:VPK/ on your Vita.
   (Don't unzip the VPK.)

2. Extract your disc with Dolphin to get its "sys" and "files" folders.
   MANUAL-PREPARE-GAME.md shows how. Then copy them so you have:
     ux0:data/mscharged/game/sys/
     ux0:data/mscharged/game/files/

3. Check that ur0:data/libshacccg.suprx is there. If not, copy your own.

4. Open the VPK in VitaShell, install it, and launch the bubble.

MANUAL-INSTALL.md has the full guide, including USB, FTP and memory card transfers.


UPDATING
--------
Just install the new VPK over the old one. Your game files, saves and
settings in ux0:data/mscharged/ are kept.
