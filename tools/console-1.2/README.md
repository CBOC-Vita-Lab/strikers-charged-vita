# Console kit for 1.3

(The folder keeps its 1.2 name, and the env files their `console-1.2` first line.)

One batched session on a Vita answers the open questions of 1.2 and 1.3: which undecided switches pay off, whether 1.3 is faster than
1.2, 1.1 and 1.0 on the same route, why Pipeline Central is slow, how the pictures change, and whether the two-Vita LAN match keeps its input delay.
The Mac drives the Vita through vitacompanion; the owner only wakes the Vita and puts it on the Wi-Fi.

**The session now takes about 3 h 40 min.** The 1.3 questions run first, so a session cut short has answered the most. It splits well after `dmid` (a little over 2 h): stop
there and `session.sh all` resumes, skipping finished runs. Heat and battery drift are in the noise through the three references `d1`, `dmid`, `d2`.

**The 1.2 defaults are the best experience, and the A/B turns each item OFF.** Prioritize Players, anti-aliasing (4x then 2x as a
ladder rung), the shadow-quad cut, lazy depth of field, sharp distant textures, the four Galaxy timing items, bounded slip and the 256-vertex
rigid merge limit are on in the build itself (`vita_plan.cpp`, "quality and timing switches"). A run's env file therefore writes the value that
switches one off: `=0`, except `MSC_DOF=1` (the game's own capture; `=0` removes the blur) and `MSC_VGX_SOFTXFORM=128` (the 1.1 limit; `=0` would
be no CPU transform at all). A run that is **slower** than the references says the default is wrong; one that is **faster** says it should come
back off. Kept off on purpose and not in the matrix: the GXM state cache, `MSC_VGX_FBO_SAMPLES`, `MSC_REDUCED_MOTION`, `MSC_LAN_INPUT_DELAY`
(its LAN leg is a separate section).

Everything here is data plus small scripts. Nothing touches `MSCN00003`. The test app is the isolated one
(`tools/stage-hardware-test.py`: title `CDXMP0001`, private root `ux0:data/mscharged-perf-test`, the extracted disc read-only).

`not-in-this-build.txt` (switches whose branch is not merged), per-run folders with `log.txt`, `status.txt`, `shots/`, `dumps/`.
Survival: per-run watchdog (stall 90 s, 150 s on stability routes), one relaunch when the app does not start, FTP retries, patience when the console drops off Wi-Fi
(8 failures x 120 s), the clock survives a restart (same command resumes; `NIGHT_RESTART=1` starts over), and the reports are written on any exit.
Measuring rule: 600 warm-up frames, 300-frame windows (the first two records of a stretch are dropped), same-night noise = 2 x the spread of the repeated 1.3 baselines;
a switch earns its place only beyond that noise and beyond 0.5 fps, with nothing else slower and no crash. `[marginal: rerun it]` marks a weak effect.
Test it without a Vita: `python3 test_kit.py` (fabricated logs from `synth.night_session`). `NIGHT_LIST=<file>` runs a trimmed list.


## Core session and the unattended full session (1.3)

`session.sh core` runs the **core session** (matrix.py tier 0, about two hours by the estimate below): 1.1 as published against 1.3's current defaults on
every scene, the proposed defaults against the current ones, screenshots of the same moments in each version, and stability (crashes, hangs, C heap,
shader compiles in play). `session.sh full` runs the core session, then tier 1, tier 2 and the automatic add-ons, the two-Vita LAN legs when `VITA_IP2`
is set, and writes the report; it never stops at one bad run (a failed swap skips that version's runs; it stops only if the console stops answering
three runs in a row, and the same command resumes). `session.sh report` writes `table.txt` (the A/B table), `core.txt` (the before/after table with p95,
p99, late pictures, picture size, GPU busy, CPU lanes, hitches and per-run stability), `looks-core.html` and `ISSUES.md` (`issues.py`: every finding by
severity with its evidence lines and a suspect switch, then a verdict per A/B switch with its Settings row).

- **Routes** (gen_routes.py): `ctour` (main menu and stadium select 330 frames each, walk-out, 30 s of play, a goal sequence), `cpipe` (Pipeline Central,
  20 s), `clava` (Lava Pit with the INFINITE power-up cheat, `MSC_CAMERA_ZOOM=1` held: items, the farthest camera, a second heavy stadium), `mega`, `crep`
  (2 minutes of a match of goals: celebrations and replays). All four new routes ran to `script exit` with every mark on the desktop harness (knobs
  66ebf5c host32 build, virtual clock).
- **Windows**: `analyse.py --core` counts a window as one full 300-frame record (`window_frames=300`): 10 s at 30 fps, 5 s at 60, 300 frame intervals
  either way (the median and p95 of 300 samples). The 10 s rule of the A/B table is unchanged. A record starts at a change of scene, so a dwell of 330
  frames holds one record.
- **One launch per route, not one per session**: the harness's `launch` needs the menus' scene manager, which does not exist during a match (tried on the
  desktop harness: `missing: sceneMgr`), and getting back to the menus after a match is not scripted. Each route boots on its own (about a minute).
- **Drift**: current-default runs at the start (block A), after the proposed block (C) and at the end (E): the proposed runs sit between A and C, the 1.1
  runs between C and E; tour and Pipeline Central have three references, the other routes two.
- **Looks**: separate launches held at 30 fps with `MSC_VITA_SHOT` (16 pictures each): `look` (menu, walk-out, open play: shadows, HUD, glow), `lookpipe`
  (mist), `lookmega`, `lookrep` (spread over the goal sequences; the contact sheet labels each picture's scene), `looksmall` (a fixed 826x468: the 1-px
  edge line and shadow static), `lookes` (`MSC_LANGUAGE=es`). `cur-setes` opens the Settings screen through the isolated app's test hook
  (`np-settings-open` in the private root, its words are the keys; `shot:NAME` writes a PPM, which `looks.py --core` converts), five pages in Spanish, then Circle.
- **Late** in core.txt = pictures shown for more refreshes than the display interval asks (`[vgx-vita] display` lines, every 600 pictures), not
  `over_33333` (a 30 fps picture lasts 33.37 ms, two refreshes at 59.94 Hz, so every one of them is "over 33,333 us").
- **Add-ons** (tier 3): `cap-tour`, `cap-pipe` (the fourth core asked for: with CapUnlocker the log says `usable`, without it `not available`) run in `full`;
  `clk500-tour` needs the owner's PSVshellPlus 500 MHz profile and runs only with `session.sh addon`.

### Version 1.1 in the isolated app

1.1 is not in the sandbox as an isolated package: the published VPK (title MSCN00003, eboot sha256 8d58eea7...) reads `ux0:data/mscharged`, the installed
game's own folder and saves, so it must never be swapped in. Its source is (the published repository at the 1.1 commit 5421c34; the later commit 4fbe675
changes documents only), and `tools/stage-hardware-test.py stage --logging --source <1.1 checkout>` stages it as the isolated CDXMP0001 logging app
(checked: 35 files rewritten, no error). The coordinator builds that tree (`NP_PLAYER=1`), packages it with the published 1.1 VPK as the donor (so it
keeps its own shader pack, `bc447993.pack`), and adds it with `prepare-versions.py --vpk 1.1=...`. The 1.1 harness knows every command and variable the
core routes use (`launch` with `powerup`, `cmode`, `MSC_FRAME_TIMING`, `MSC_VITA_SHOT`, `MSC_CAMERA_ZOOM`, `MSC_VITA_RENDER`); it does not have
`[lane]` with the game/send columns or `[hitch]` lines, so those columns are 1.3-only. Version `1.3p` is `final/strikers-13-final-proposed.vpk`
(`prepare-versions.py --vpk 1.3p=...`).

### How the existing tools fit

- **`tools/stage-hardware-test.py`** (`stage`, `package`, `verify`) makes the isolated test app: it rewrites every
  `ux0:data/mscharged` path to `...-perf-test`, adds `np_hardware_isolation.h` (an env line is accepted only if it holds no `/`, `:` or
  `..`, except the path keys such as `MSC_INPUT_SCRIPT`, which must sit under the private root) and pins `NP_DATA_DIR`, the disc,
  `NP_IMPORT_SAVE=0` and the virtual-time switches. `--logging` (new) builds with `NP_LOGGING=1`; see "Build" below.
- **`np-player.txt`** is read once at start by `LoadEnvFile()` (`np_vita_main.cpp`): `NAME=VALUE` lines, `#` comments, at most 96 lines
  and 16384 bytes, at `ux0:data/mscharged/np-player.txt` (in the isolated app `.../mscharged-perf-test/np-player.txt`). The
  Settings screen writes its rows between two marker lines of the same file; the kit's files carry no marker lines and replace the whole file.
  Every value in `env/*.txt` was checked against `np_settings.cpp`, `docs/TECHNICAL.md` and the reader of the key (`test_kit.py`).
- **The unattended navigation scripts** (`native/platform/vita/session/nav/match60.txt`, and the older `nav_kickoff.txt`/`nav_match60.txt`
  of the unattended kit) are the harness script language: `wait`, `if`, `try`, `hold`, `press`, `mash`, `vchase`, `log`, `exit`. The harness
  reads it on the Vita through `MSC_INPUT_SCRIPT` (`np_harness.cpp`). `gen_routes.py` builds the kit's routes from match60 and adds the
  `log @begin <scene>` / `log @end <scene>` marks the analysis keys on.
- **`session_np2.sh` / `install_promote.sh`** (vitacompanion: FTP port 1337, commands port 1338, `launch`, `promote`) are the older

## Build (the coordinator's part)

The isolated app has to be a **logging** build, or the log stays empty. `stage-hardware-test.py stage` without `--logging` writes
`NP_LOGGING := 0`, and with that the Vita build silences stdio: no `[frame-timing]`, `[run]`, `[fps]` or `[vgx]` lines at all.

1. For each of `v1.3` (the working head), `v1.2`, `v1.1`, `v1.0`: `python3 tools/stage-hardware-test.py stage --logging --source <snapshot> --destination <new dir>`.
2. Build each with `NP_PLAYER=1` (the staged makefile pins the rest), package with the tool's `package` (donor = the public VPK of that
   version, so each carries its own shader pack), and keep the recorded eboot SHA-256 of each.
3. `python3 tools/console-1.2/prepare-versions.py --out versions --vpk 1.3=... --vpk 1.2=... --vpk 1.1=... --vpk 1.0=... [--sha256 1.3=...]`.

Same compiler flags and logging level for all four (the validation spec asks for that); the absolute numbers carry the logging build's
small extra cost, the deltas between runs do not.

## LAN: two Vitas, separate short section

Both Vitas on the same Wi-Fi (`MSC_LAN_MODE=wifi`, the Settings row MULTIPLAYER LINK > SAME WI-FI) so the Mac keeps its FTP link to both
during the match. The nearby (ad hoc) default drops the Vita's Wi-Fi and shows the system dialog; it is not in the session (to try it: set

1. `export VITA_HOST=<host Vita> VITA_HOST2=<joining Vita>`, `bash session.sh stage` once for each (`VITA_HOST=<other> bash session.sh stage`).
2. `bash session.sh lan lan-base`: puts `lan/lan-base-host.txt` / `-join.txt` as `np-player.txt`, launches the host, 12 s later the joiner. The

4. `report` prints, per console, the `[mp-lan]` input-delay lines, any DESYNC / connection lost line, and the play-window frame times.

## Reading the table

`analyse.py <session dir>` (or `session.sh report`) prints one block per scene and one row per run:
`p50 / p95 / p99` frame time in ms and fps, each with the delta against the 1.2 defaults. Rules:

- **Windows**: 10 s steady windows built from consecutive 300-frame `[frame-timing]` records of the same scene (several records at 60 fps).
  A scene that never lasts 10 s (goal sequence, replay) is pooled over the run and marked `~`.
- **Compiles**: a window is dropped if its frames contain a `[vgx] ... shader compiles` or `[vgx-vita] ... came N ms after ... +N shaders` line,
  or lie in a 600-present span where the `shader work so far` counter rose without such a line (`--keep-suspect` keeps those).
- **Noise** = 2 x the spread (difference) of the two reference runs for that scene, never less than 0.25 ms (p50), 0.5 ms (p95), 1.0 ms (p99), 0.3 fps.
  A delta beyond it is marked `*` (better) or `!` (worse); inside it nothing is claimed.
- The `== runs` block lists clocks (a run not at 444/222/222 MHz is flagged), frames, windows lost to compiles, AUTO 30/60 switches, and
  runs to repeat.
- The header lines a run prints (`[run] fps=..., dynres=..., settings: ...`) show that a switch took effect: `analyse.py --header`.

## Known risks and unverified parts

- Nothing in `session.sh` has run against a real Vita; it is tested against a fake console. The FTP delete of the old log is best effort (the
  log is cut at its last header anyway).
- Swapping `eboot.bin` and `shadercache/` over FTP has not been tried on this app. Fallback: VitaShell installs.
- The save file in `np-data` is shared by all versions of the test app; the first launch of a fresh private root shows "create save data",
  which the routes accept. A save written by 1.2 may or may not load in 1.0; the routes need none.
- Estimated minutes are the desktop harness's frame counts at 30 fps plus about a minute of start-up and pull; `session-times.csv` replaces them
  with measured ones as the session goes.
- The `mega` route could not be checked on the desktop harness: it selects the Mega Saves lesson by touch (`touch 445 412`), which the harness
  does not simulate, so it ends in an ordinary match there. The script is the earlier console-proven one with a longer touch hold at the end.
  `tour` and `look` were run through to their `script exit` on the harness; the LAN scripts through `native_pair.py`.
- Who scores the goal is not controlled: the CPU's goals count, and the number of goal sequences varies by run (the `goal:` rows are
  pooled over however many played).
- The LAN scripts are the host pair test's; they were run here on the desktop harness only. The radio dialog (ad hoc) is not exercised.
- Polling the log over FTP late in the route costs a few frames of the closing idle time, never a measured scene.
